Emphasis and strengthened text

When creating a website, it is important to communicate clearly. We can also emphasize certain words to help readers understand our message.

... Separating content from presentation

Explanation

The "strong" and "em" elements are better choices when expressing importance or emphasis because they add meaning to the text, rather than simply changing its appearance. Strong indicates importance, while em adds emphasis. This semantic meaning can also help assistive technologies interpret content. The b and i elements are used for different kinds of visual distinctions.
